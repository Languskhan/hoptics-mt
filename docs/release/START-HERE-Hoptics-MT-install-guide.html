Hoptics MT · v0.3.0

Install & test guide

Hoptics MT is a new screen for Meshtastic. Thanks for trying it out! Installing takes a few minutes, all from a web browser.

Check first:
  • Hoptics MT runs on two Heltec kits with the color touch screen, the Heltec V4 Expansion Kit and the Heltec V4-R8 Expansion Kit V2, and (beta, new in v0.3.0) on the LilyGO T-Deck Plus and the Elecrow ThinkNode M9. Each needs its own files (below). On a bare Heltec board or one with the small OLED screen, the display won't work.
  • You need a computer with Chrome or Edge. Phones, Safari and Firefox can't flash firmware.
  • Use a USB-C cable that carries data. Some cables only charge.

1Easiest: the one-click installer

  1. Plug the kit into the computer and open languskhan.github.io/hoptics-mt in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Pick your radio in the list, click Connect and install and pick its port when Chrome asks. It's often listed as USB JTAG/serial debug unit (the M9 shows as a USB-SERIAL CH340 COM port).
  3. Click Install. When it asks about erasing:
    • First time on Hoptics (coming from regular Meshtastic, MeshCore or other firmware, or a new board): tick Erase. You'll set the region again in the setup wizard.
    • Updating Hoptics: leave Erase unticked. Everything is kept, including the screen's own settings.
  4. Wait about a minute until it says it's done. The files come from the latest release, so there's nothing to download.
T-Deck Plus: if the screen stays dark after installing, slide the power switch off and on.
ThinkNode M9: its first start updates the LoRa chip (about 10 seconds); keep it plugged in. It has no touch screen: the arrows move the highlight, the center key presses, Back goes back.
Both have a short guide of their own: Hoptics-MT-T-Deck-Plus-M9-Guide-v0.3.0.pdf on the release page.
If that page doesn't work for you, use one of the two ways below instead. They do the same thing with files you download yourself.

Other ways to install

Way A
Your kit already runs regular Meshtastic (version 2.6 or newer).
Uses the Meshtastic Web Flasher. Your channels, name and settings are kept.
Way B
Anything else: it runs MeshCore or other firmware, it's brand new, you aren't sure of the version, or Way A didn't work.
Uses Espressif's ESP Tool page. Works every time.
If the browser can't connect to the kit in either way, put it in boot mode: hold the USER (PRG) button, tap RST, then let go of USER. Then try connecting again. Once flashing finishes, tap RST to start it normally. (T-Deck Plus: hold the trackball down while sliding the power switch on; afterwards switch it off and on. ThinkNode M9: it normally connects without this.)

Way A: Meshtastic Web Flasher (keeps your settings)

  1. Plug the kit into the computer and open flasher.meshtastic.org in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Under Select Target Device, pick Heltec V4, Heltec V4 R8, T-Deck or ThinkNode M9, or let it auto-detect.
  3. Click the small folder icon next to Select Firmware. Choose the .update.bin for your kit.
  4. Click Flash. Leave Full Erase and Install off; it may already be greyed out, which is fine. Click Update, then pick the kit's port when Chrome asks.
  5. Wait for it to finish. If the screen doesn't restart on its own, tap RST.
Use the .update.bin file here, not the .factory.bin. The Meshtastic flasher can only do a full install with official release files.

Way B: ESP Tool page

  1. Plug in the kit and open espressif.github.io/esptool-js in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Connect and pick the kit's port. It's often listed as USB JTAG/serial debug unit or a COM port. Wait for "Connected to device: ESP32-S3".
  3. In the Program section, change Flash Address to 0x0. Click Choose File and pick the .factory.bin for your kit.
  4. Click Add File. In the new row, type 0x650000 and choose mt-esp32s3-ota.bin.
  5. Set the Erase all box:
    • Coming from regular Meshtastic: leave it unchecked. Your channels and settings are kept.
    • Coming from MeshCore or other firmware, or a new board: check it. This wipes the radio clean first.
  6. Click Program. It takes about a minute. It's done when the log ends with "Hash of data verified."
  7. Click Disconnect, then tap RST, or unplug the kit and plug it back in.
If it fails partway through, set Baudrate to 115200 before clicking Connect and try again.

2Set it up

  • If the radio was wiped or is new, the screen walks you through 3 steps: region (pick US), name, and look. The radio stays silent until a region is set.
  • Wi-Fi (needed for the street map): go to Settings → WI-FI, turn it on, and enter your network name and password. Only 2.4 GHz networks work.
On this radio, Wi-Fi turns Bluetooth off, so the Meshtastic phone app can't connect over Bluetooth while Wi-Fi is on. Turn Wi-Fi off again to get Bluetooth back.

3What to test

Tick these off as you go. They don't save, so jot down anything odd.

4Report how it went

The easiest way: Settings → DEVICE → Report a problem, scan the QR code, and add what happened. If the radio ever crashes, a Crash report screen with its own QR code appears by itself after it restarts.

Or send this to whoever shared the build with you, or open an issue at github.com/Languskhan/hoptics-mt/issues:

  • which way you installed it (A or B)
  • what worked and what didn't
  • for anything that went wrong: what you were doing, roughly what time, and a photo of the screen

If it keeps restarting over and over, unplug it and report it right away.

5Going back to regular Meshtastic

Any time: open flasher.meshtastic.org, pick your kit and a release, click Flash, then choose Full Erase and Install. This wipes the radio, so you'll set the region again afterwards.
