Flash Hoptics from your browserBETA

Three steps, about a minute. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and a USB-C cable that carries data.

This browser can't flash firmware

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS). Firefox, Safari, phones and tablets can't talk to USB devices this way. You can still download the files.

1Pick your node

Loading nodes…

Not sure which Heltec kit you have? Check the board: if it says V4-R8, pick the V4-R8 kit.

2New install or update?

First time on Hoptics
When the installer asks, tick Erase device. You get a clean start and fresh encryption keys.
Updating Hoptics
Leave Erase unticked. Your settings, nodes, messages and pairings are kept.

3Connect and flash

Plug the node in, click the button, and choose the port that appears (often "USB JTAG/serial debug unit").

Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

This node is flashed by copying a file, no installer needed:

  1. Download the firmware file.
  2. Double-tap the node's reset button. A drive appears on your computer.
  3. Drag the file onto that drive. The node restarts on its own.

Can't connect?

  1. Try another USB-C cable. Some only charge.
  2. Hold USER, tap RST, let go of USER, then try again.
  3. Close other apps that use the port, like the Meshtastic web client.

Beta firmware

Hoptics is still in beta. It works day to day, but bugs are expected. You can always go back to stock Meshtastic with flasher.meshtastic.org.

After flashing

The setup wizard asks for your region first. The radio stays silent until you pick one.

Rather do it by hand?

Download the factory or update image from the firmware list and flash it with esptool. The README has the commands.
